CheerUp! Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. CheerUp! is a casual entertainment and arcade mini-game application designed to bring fun, positive breaks into your day. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CheerUp!.

1. Information We Collect

CheerUp! does not operate user accounts, registration, or profiles. We do not directly collect, store, sell, or rent personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or contact list.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CheerUp! uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements. These include:

  • Optional Rewarded Ads: In arcade games (Tap, Rush, Gravity, and Ping Pong), players can choose to watch a rewarded video advertisement on the Game Over screen to continue their session and score.
  • Wheels Intermissions: In the Wheels section, a short video ad is shown after every 5 spins before returning directly to the active challenge card.

To deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements and prevent fraud, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device data, such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA, subject to your tracking permission)
  • IP address and general geographic location (country/city level)
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and mobile network information
  • Ad interaction, viewability, and diagnostic performance metrics

Google's use of data collected through AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google handles ad data and how to manage your privacy choices here:

Google Advertising Privacy & Technologies
Google Privacy Policy

3. Apple Game Center

CheerUp! offers optional leaderboard functionality through Apple Game Center.

If you choose to participate in leaderboards, Apple may process information associated with your Game Center account, including your player identity and high scores. This data is handled directly by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy:

Apple's Privacy Policy

4. Local Notifications (Play Reminders)

CheerUp! includes an optional reminder notification scheduled every two days to encourage casual play breaks.

All notifications are scheduled and managed locally on your device using iOS local notification APIs. We do not use remote push servers, and no personal data or device tokens are transmitted to any server for notifications. You can enable or disable play reminders at any time in the app's Settings or through your device's iOS Settings.

5. Data Storage & Preferences

Your gameplay statistics, high scores, unlocked preferences, language selection, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). CheerUp! does not maintain remote user databases.

6. Children's Privacy

CheerUp! does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). All third-party services (Google AdMob and Apple Game Center) are configured in compliance with applicable children's privacy laws and platform parental controls.

7. Data Security

Because CheerUp! does not collect or transmit personal information to its own servers, your personal data is not stored or maintained by us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app, features, or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will always be posted on this page with the latest effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support link available on the CheerUp! App Store product page.
