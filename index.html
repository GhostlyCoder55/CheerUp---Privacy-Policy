CheerUp! Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. CheerUp! is a casual entertainment and mini-game application designed to bring a little fun into your day.

1. Information We Collect

CheerUp! does not directly collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not require accounts, registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal profiles.

2. Apple Game Center

CheerUp! offers optional leaderboard functionality through Apple Game Center.

If you participate in leaderboards, Apple may process information associated with your Game Center account, including your player identity and submitted game scores.

This information is handled by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Apple's Privacy Policy

3. Data Storage

Gameplay information and app preferences may be stored locally on your device. CheerUp! does not operate its own user database or account-management system.

4. Third-Party Services

CheerUp! does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Apple Game Center is used for optional leaderboard functionality.

5. Children's Privacy

CheerUp! does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Apple Game Center features are subject to Apple's applicable age requirements and parental controls.

6. Data Security

CheerUp! does not maintain personal information on its own servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the support information provided on the CheerUp! App Store listing.
